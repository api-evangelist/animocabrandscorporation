## Business Model: Build, Invest, Accelerate
Animoca Brands operates through three integrated pillars:

- **Build** — Creates consumer platforms (e.g., Moca Network, The Sandbox, Open Campus, Anichess) and institutional infrastructure (e.g., Anchorpoint Financial with HKMA stablecoin issuer sandbox license approved April 2026).
- **Invest** — Manages over 600 strategic portfolio positions leveraging its network effects to compound value across the ecosystem.
- **Accelerate** — Provides institutional-grade token strategy, research, go-to-market support, and liquidity provisioning through its digital asset services to launch and scale decentralised networks.

## What Animoca Brands Stands For
Animoca Brands is dedicated to reimagining future economies by building, investing in, and accelerating impactful technologies that turn participants into stakeholders. This conviction governs a tripartite strategy: consumer platforms provide ownership experiences, institutional infrastructure bridges decentralized ecosystems with traditional finance, and a 600+ company portfolio expands global reach.

## Key Terms

### Business Model Terms
- Investor-Operator Flywheel: An operating model where a company simultaneously builds products, invests in companies, and accelerates their growth — creating a self-reinforcing network where each activity compounds the value of the others. This is Animoca Brands' core operating model.
- Digital Property Rights: The principle that users should truly own their digital assets, enabling open and participatory economies. This is Animoca Brands' founding mission.

### Technology Terms
- Web3: The next evolution of the internet built on decentralised protocols, enabling user ownership and digital property rights. Animoca Brands is one of the largest investor-operators in Web3.
- Agentic AI: AI systems that can autonomously perform tasks, make decisions, and take actions on behalf of users. Animoca Brands operates in this space via Minds by Animoca Brands.
- DeFi (Decentralised Finance): Financial services built on blockchain that operate without traditional intermediaries such as banks or brokerages.
- Tokenomics: The economic design and mechanics of a blockchain token, including supply, distribution, utility, and governance. Animoca Brands provides tokenomics advisory as part of its Digital Asset Services.

### Product-Specific Terms
- RWA (Real World Assets): Traditional assets such as currencies, bonds, and commodities represented as tokens on blockchain networks. Animoca Brands operates in this space via NUVA and Anchorpoint Financial.
- EduFi: Education Finance — on-chain financial infrastructure enabling funding, credentialing, and economic participation in education. Open Campus operates in this space.
- HKDAP: Hong Kong dollar-backed stablecoin developed by Anchorpoint Financial under the HKMA stablecoin issuer sandbox framework.
